# Fiverr gigs: ready to paste

Set up: fiverr.com → Become a Seller → fill in your profile → Create a new gig (repeat for each gig).
Images are in this folder: `gig1_kdp_formatting.png`, `gig2_pattern_pages.png`, `gig3_kdp_cover.png` (1280x769, Fiverr's size).

Prices are set low on purpose: your first 5–10 reviews matter more than profit. Raise them by ~30% after 10 five-star reviews.
Fiverr keeps 20% of each order. New sellers' earnings usually clear after about 2 weeks.

## Handling an order (you + me)
1. When an order arrives, copy the buyer's requirements and put their files in a new folder, e.g. `coloring_book/orders/<buyer>/`.
2. Open a session and tell me "new Fiverr order in orders/<buyer>". I build the files and check them against KDP's rules.
3. You check the preview and deliver it on Fiverr.
4. Ask happy buyers to leave a review. Early reviews decide how fast you grow.

## Getting the first orders
- Turn on all three gigs, and go online in the Fiverr app daily (active sellers rank higher).
- Reply to messages within an hour: response time is a ranking factor.
- Post your gig images in KDP Facebook groups and on Pinterest. Don't spam; share your work when it's relevant.
- Never move a buyer off Fiverr for payment: it gets accounts banned.
