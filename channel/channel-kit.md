## Description (updated 21 Nov 2026)
Dustlight: stories about the things we forgot. 🕯️

Tiny 3D stories about the people and moments that stay with us: a plate set every Thanksgiving, a swing pushed every evening, a cup of tea made for someone who isn't there. Each one is under a minute and has a twist you won't see coming.

🎬 New story every few days

## Style
- Warm, dusty 3D toy world. A bulb glow, floating dust, a soft vignette and grain.
- Narrator: ElevenLabs "George", a warm British storyteller.
- Captions: 1 to 3 words at a time, white with a thick black outline, keywords in yellow (#FFD640), centred around y=1210 to 1240 of 1920.
- Cut every 2 to 3 s. End card text on black.
- Each story: a strong hook in the first 3 s, an unanswered question every few seconds, a payoff that fits the format (emotional, funny, surprising or a cliffhanger), and a pinned comment that asks a question.
