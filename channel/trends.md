## Top 3 for Dustlight
1. **Holiday run-up (24).** A Thanksgiving or first-snow story about the chair nobody sits in any more, or the toy train that comes out once a year. It fits the "things we forgot" line, and the kitchen, snowy street and toys are already in `world.py` and `scene.py`.
2. **Numbered parts with a cliffhanger (23).** The format already worked for the Lost Toy Room. A 3-part holiday story released a day apart, with each part ending on a question for the viewer.
3. **Toy nostalgia, combined with #1 (22).** "The toy train that only runs on Christmas Eve." Toys are the channel's strongest set.

Watch out for #3 in the table: many channels now use the same Claude-made 3D story look. Dustlight stands out through its own characters (peg dolls, Bun-Bun), synthesized sound and the "forgotten things" angle, not through the tool.
