**New this afternoon:** the 3D animated-story format is getting crowded. Free AI tools for it launched on 1 Nov and tutorials followed on 12, 15 and 18 Nov, so many lookalike channels are coming. Originality room for row 2 drops from 4 to 3. Dustlight's edge is the hand-built look (the new low-poly night style), real recorded sound and emotional twist endings, not the format itself.

Other checks: hook guides put the Shorts hook deadline at about 2 s (revid.ai, 6 Nov). Google Trends rising US searches this week are sports, the World Cup bracket and shopping, with no fit for the channel.

Left out: pranks, dance challenges, Black Friday shopping (no fit for the brand, or brand or safety issues). The trackers disagree with each other, so the top-10 lists (veedio, kabo, trends24, shortstrends) are noted but not ranked.

## Top 3 for Dustlight
1. **Holiday kickoff:** a sad-start, warm-twist Christmas or Advent story. For example: one empty chair at the Thanksgiving table that turns out to be kept for someone coming home, or an advent calendar someone keeps opening for a person who has gone. Build it this week so it lands 26 to 29 Nov.
2. **Animated story Shorts:** this is already our format, and it is still growing, but copycats are arriving fast. Stand out on look and sound: the low-poly night style, real recorded foley, and a typed hook headline in the first 2 s.
3. **Soft-launch reveal:** structure a story as a slow reveal of *who* someone is waiting or caring for. The viewer sees hands, objects and an empty seat, and the face only at the end. It fits our twist endings. It's short-lived, so only use it as the structure, not the music.
