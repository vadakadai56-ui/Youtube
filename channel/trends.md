## Top 3 for Dustlight
1. **Holiday kickoff:** a sad-start, warm-twist Christmas or Advent story. For example: one empty chair at the Thanksgiving table that turns out to be kept for someone coming home, or an advent calendar someone keeps opening for a person who has gone. Build it this week so it lands 26 to 29 Nov.
2. **Animated story Shorts:** this is already our format, and the evidence says it's still growing. Keep the 55 to 75 s length, the hook in 2 s, and an open question every few seconds. Push "based on a feeling everyone has" titles.
3. **Soft-launch reveal:** structure a story as a slow reveal of *who* someone is waiting or caring for. The viewer sees hands, objects and an empty seat, and the face only at the end. It fits our twist endings. It's short-lived, so only use it as the structure, not the music.
