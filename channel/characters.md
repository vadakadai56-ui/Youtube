# Character names (owner's rule, 23 Nov 2026)

Every important character in every video gets a name, chosen by Claude without asking. That includes the main person,
anyone the story turns on, and pets that matter. Background extras do not need names.

How to name them:
- **Short and easy to say and read in a caption:** one or two syllables (Walter, Rose, Milo, Nell, Biscuit).
- **Fits the person and the story's world:** age, era, place. A 1956 grandmother is not a "Kayden".
- **Varied:** different initials within one video, a mix of backgrounds across videos, and no repeating a main name
  within a month unless the character comes back (a series or a running character).
- **Said in the story:** it goes in the narration, usually in the first two lines ("Every morning, Walter makes two cups
  of tea"), and in the title, caption or first comment when it helps.
- **"You" or "I" stories:** the narrator can stay unnamed, but the people around them get names.
- **Real people:** only for real facts (Hermann Ebbinghaus, Daniel Kahneman), never for invented roles.
- Add every new name to the list below when the script is written.
