Somebody kept this lens.

A brass cinema projector lens, built here entirely in code and lit like a photo studio. Nothing on this page is a video. Scroll to take it apart.

f/1.9 · 75 mm · Ø 66 mm · 8 parts
Close-up · time runs at 1/12

Four pieces of glass.

Each element carries a thin coating that tints its reflections violet and green. The dust in front of it is drifting at one twelfth of its real speed.

Eight parts. No screws.

The rings thread onto the barrel. Take them off in order, front ring first, and the glass lifts straight out.

Turned brass

Cut on a lathe.

Every part is one profile spun around the lens axis, the way a machinist cut the real thing. Move your cursor to turn the studio lights.
