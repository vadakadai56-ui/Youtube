# Brag Plan: Domino's Riga 2+1 ad pitch

INTERNAL. Made for one viewer, the store's MD. Not for posting until the MD approves the ad.

## What is this app?
A 15 s vertical motion-design ad for Domino's Riga's running 2+1 deal (code `MORE`), built as code from the
dominospizza.lv site's own logo, pizza photos and deal copy, pitched by a makeline team member.

## The angle
The Domino's logo has always shown the deal: two dots on the blue half, one on the red half. 2+1. The brag
video makes the MD notice that first, then shows the ad that was built on it.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The real domino tile, alone on dark navy, with one line: "Look closely at our logo." A "2" settles beside the
blue half and a "+1" beside the red half, on the beat.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The deal as it runs today: the site's own 2+1 banner ("Pērc 2 picas un saņem 1 dāvanā!", code MORE).
- The ad itself playing in a phone: dots become pizzas, the camera dives into the blue half, pans into the red one.
- Three facts arriving one by one: one camera with no cuts; logo, photos and deal taken from dominospizza.lv; 15 s, 9:16, with sound.

## Outro / punchline
The logo settles with "2+1 · kods MORE", then "Ready when you are." (connective line, asserts nothing).

## User flow worth showing
The ad's own flow: three dots → tile → dive into the blue half (2 pizzas) → pan to the red half (gift pizza)
→ pull back into the logo → code MORE. Shown as the real rendered ad inside a phone.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet agency pitch reel for one person, the MD
- Interpretation: few scenes, long holds, mixed case, soft transitions caused by the tile; nothing loud.

## Format: vertical — 1080x1920
## Duration: 20 s

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #04121F to #0E3354 (the ad's radial navy)
- Accent: #E31837 (Domino's red), #006491 (Domino's blue)
- Text: #FFFFFF
- Display font: Montserrat 900 / 800 (the ad's stand-in; the site's Gotham Pro lacks Latvian letters)
- Body font: Montserrat 600
- Strongest visual element: the domino tile from the official logo PNG

## Share copy (draft)
For the MD: a 15-second 2+1 ad built from our own logo, the dots were always 2+1. Code MORE, ready for Reels and TikTok.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, sparse professional accents
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-12 (ende.app, CC BY 4.0), 110 BPM, steady and clean
- Music treatment: 0.32 volume, fade in 0.4 s, fade out over the last 1.5 s
- Music cue guidance: preset read. Strong cues 8.74, 13.11, 17.47 s. Scene seams on beats 3.27, 7.09, 13.11, 17.47.
  Fact chips on every other beat from 13.64 (13.64, 14.73, 15.84), each held to the end of the scene.
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the glow behind the tile and the phone breathes with music RMS. No visualiser.
- SFX posture: sparse, warm, low high-frequency risk
- Audio-coupled moments: the "2" and "+1" landing; the banner arriving; each fact chip; the logo landing.
- Restraint rule: no hits on every cut, nothing bright or repeated loudly; the ad clip plays muted.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Hook — 3.27 s
The official domino tile, centred, on navy. "Look closely at our logo." types word by word above it.
"2" lands left of the blue half at 1.64 s, "+1" right of the red half at 2.19 s.
Sequential/interaction: yes — the two numbers arrive one after the other.
Audio intent: curiosity. Audio-coupled idea: two soft drops on the numbers.
Transition mood: soft → the tile slides up and shrinks as the banner arrives.

### Scene 2 — The deal we already run — 3.82 s
"It's literally our deal." over the site's real 2+1 banner (Dominos_21_1440x600_LV.jpg), "Atlaides kods MORE" pill below.
Sequential/interaction: banner, then pill.
Audio intent: recognition. Audio-coupled idea: one card-place sound on the banner.
Transition mood: soft → the banner scales into a phone screen.

### Scene 3 — The ad — 6.02 s
"So I made it the ad." Phone frame centred, playing the real ad from 3.0 s to 9.0 s (dive, buy 2, pan, gift).
Sequential/interaction: the ad plays.
Audio intent: let the music carry. Audio-coupled idea: none.
Transition mood: clean → the phone stays; facts arrive around it.

### Scene 4 — How it's built — 4.36 s
The phone plays the ad's ending (11.4 s to 15.0 s): logo and code MORE. Three fact chips arrive one by one:
"One camera. No cuts." / "Logo, photos and deal from dominospizza.lv" / "15 s · 9:16 · with sound".
Sequential/interaction: yes — three chips on every other beat, held together afterwards.
Audio intent: quiet confidence. Audio-coupled idea: soft click per chip.
Transition mood: soft → the phone dissolves into the logo.

### Scene 5 — Outro — 2.53 s
The official Domino's logo, "2+1 · kods MORE", then "Ready when you are." Small "Internal pitch" line.
Audio intent: settle. Audio-coupled idea: one warm bell when the logo lands (17.47 s strong cue).

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, clean
**Audio summary:** a steady warm bed from the first frame, a few soft drops and clicks on arrivals, one bell on the logo, fade out.
