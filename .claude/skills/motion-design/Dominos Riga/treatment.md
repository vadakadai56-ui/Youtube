# "Always 2+1": director's treatment

Domino's Riga · 2+1 deal (code MORE) · 15 s · 9:16 · Reels, TikTok, Stories
Internal pitch draft. Not approved by Domino's.

## The idea
We never had to invent this offer. It's been printed on every box, every receipt and every driver's jacket for decades.
Two dots on the blue half, one on the red. **2+1.** The ad lets people discover it the way you'd notice it yourself:
slowly, then all at once.

## The feeling
A late-night food ad with the calm of a watch commercial. Nothing shouts. The camera never cuts, and it always moves.
The pizza is the only warm, messy thing in a clean graphic world, so it looks even hotter by contrast.

**Three words:** precise, warm, inevitable.

## The world
- **Ground:** deep night navy, `#04121F` at the edges and `#0E3354` in the middle, like a dark kitchen with one light on.
- **Brand:** Domino's blue `#006491`, red `#E31837`, white. No other colours except the food.
- **Light:** one soft light from top left. Every plate and tile casts the same soft shadow down and right.
- **Type:** Montserrat Black for lines, ExtraBold for the code. Sentence case, short lines. Words arrive one by one,
  from blurred to sharp. No bounces.
- **Camera:** one continuous move for all 15 s. Fast at first, then long and slow. It only zooms in until the pull-back,
  then only zooms out, and it turns one way only.

## Sound
- **Music:** bright, warm pop at about 108–110 BPM. Quiet for the first two seconds so the three taps own the opening, then full energy from the colour flood. It resolves on the logo.
- **Signature sound:** the domino tap. It opens the film, and a softer version comes back when the pizzas return to dots.
- **Mix:** effects sit under the music, nothing harsh. Phone-speaker test before approval.
- No voiceover. The ad must work muted, and most people will watch it muted.

## Optional: real food from our own store
The one thing a code-built ad can't fake is real food. If the store manager agrees, film 4–6 vertical clips on the
make line: dough being stretched, the sauce spiral, the cheese drop, a pizza coming out of the oven, a box closing. 3–5 s each, on a phone, at 60 fps if possible.
They'd play **inside the dots** in shot 4, so each circle becomes a window into the real kitchen just before it becomes a pizza.

## What it needs before it can run
| Need | Why |
|---|---|
| MD approval, and the franchise's brand sign-off for paid use | Running it needs brand sign-off, not just the MD's OK |
| A native Latvian speaker to check shot 6's line | It's the only line not taken from Domino's own copy |
| The official brand font with Latvian letters | The site's Gotham Pro files lack ā, ņ and ū, so Montserrat stands in |
| High-resolution pizza photos from marketing | The site's photos are 480 px, fine for 1080p but soft for close-ups or 4K |
| Confirmation that the 2+1 terms are current | The ad repeats the site's conditions word for word |

## What it must never do
- Use any claim that isn't on dominospizza.lv: no invented delivery times, no "best pizza" lines.
- Cut. The whole film is one camera move.
- Bounce, flash or shake. Its confidence comes from staying calm.
