# /yt-hook: the first 3 seconds

Most viewers decide in the first 1 to 3 s. The hook is the first spoken line, the first caption and the first picture at once.

## Write 10 variants, across these patterns
1. **Strange routine:** "Every morning he makes two cups of tea."
2. **Number or time:** "For one hundred and twelve days, at six o'clock..."
3. **Rule:** "The lost toy room has one rule."
4. **Hidden place:** "Inside a snow globe in your grandma's attic..."
5. **Direct address:** "Every toy you ever lost is waiting in this room."
6. **Reaction first:** "The other parents pulled their children away."
7. **Mid-action:** start in the middle of the moment, no setup.
Mix in your own. Each under 12 words, speakable in under 3 s.

## Score each 1 to 5 on
- **Specific** (a concrete image, not an abstraction)
- **Question** (does it force "why?")
- **Visual** (can frame 1 show it?)
- **Honest** (the story actually pays it off; no clickbait the ending betrays)
- **Brand** (Dustlight tone: quiet, warm, a little sad)

Return a table sorted by total, recommend one, and say what frame 1 should show. If it replaces the script's first line, update `narration.txt` and flag that the owner must re-approve the change.
