Every Thanksgiving... for nine years... Margaret sets one extra plate.
Same table. Same chair. Same little name card.
"Danny."
The first year, the family asked who it was for.
The second year... they stopped asking.
Her son left after a fight. She said something she can't take back.
So every November, she mails him one postcard. Five words.
[softly] "Your plate is still warm."
He never writes back.
Year six, she hears he has a little girl. She's never met her.
Year eight... she buys a tiny chair. Just in case.
Year nine... the turkey goes cold. The plate stays empty.
Year ten... she almost doesn't set it.
Then... a knock.
A little girl walks past everyone... straight to the tiny chair.
She picks up the name card. [whispering] "Danny... that's my daddy."
And in the doorway... holding nine postcards... stands her son.
[softly] "Mom... is my plate still warm?"
She doesn't say a word. She just sets one more plate.
Now every Thanksgiving, Margaret sets the table... and every chair is full.
