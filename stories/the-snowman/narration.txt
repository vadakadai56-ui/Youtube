Every December first, a snowman appears outside Mrs Kowalski's window.
Nobody has ever seen who builds it.
Twelve years. Same spot. Same red scarf.
The neighbours have theories. The postman. A ghost. Mrs Kowalski herself.
My brother Oscar set up a camera. By morning, snow had covered the lens.
So this year... I didn't sleep.
At four a.m., the bin lorry stopped outside her house.
Danny the bin man jumped down. Three balls of snow. One red scarf. Three minutes.
Then he was gone.
The next morning, I asked him why.
[softly] "When I was eight, her husband built one for me. I was the kid whose dad never came home."
[softly] "He made me promise. One every year. For her."
At the window, Mrs Kowalski waved.
And Danny, in the bin lorry... waved back.
Who would you build one for?
