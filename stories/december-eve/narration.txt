The night before December... my mum becomes a different person.
At six, the boxes come down from the attic.
At seven, the tree is up. Crooked.
At eight, every light in the house is flashing.
At nine, the cat is wearing a hat.
At ten, Dad is wearing a hat.
And at midnight... she whispers, [whispering] "It's December."
The whole street goes dark... because our house just used all the power.
