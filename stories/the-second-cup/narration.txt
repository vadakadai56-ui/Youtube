Every morning for three years, Walter makes two cups of tea.
One for him. And one for Rose.
Rose's chair is always empty.
And every morning... her tea goes cold.
The neighbours whisper. [whispering] "Poor Walter. He still thinks she's coming home."
But every morning at nine... Walter pours Rose's tea into a flask...
puts on his coat... and walks two miles in the cold.
Nobody ever asked where he was going.
He goes to a quiet house with a garden. Sunny Meadows Care Home.
Rose is sitting by the window. She doesn't know who he is anymore.
Walter sits down. [warmly] "Good morning. I brought you some tea."
She smiles. "You're very kind. Have we met?"
[softly] "Not yet," he says.
Every day, Rose forgets him.
And every day... Walter gets to make her fall in love with him again.
Later, the nurse asks Rose about her visitor.
Rose blushes. [warmly] "I don't know who he is... but I think I'm going to marry him one day."
