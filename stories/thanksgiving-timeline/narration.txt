Thanksgiving in my house. A timeline.
Six a.m. Mum is already yelling at the turkey.
Nine a.m. Dad says he's got the pie. He does not have the pie.
One p.m. The smoke alarm agrees with Mum.
Five p.m. Everyone's starving. The turkey? Still frozen.
Six p.m. Grandma walks in. Shop-bought pie. A roast chicken.
Grandma wins Thanksgiving. Again.
