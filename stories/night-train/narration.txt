Every night at three minutes past three, a tiny train takes lost things home.
You don't need a ticket. You need a memory.
A glove remembers a cold hand. A teddy remembers a bedtime. A key remembers a door.
Tonight, the conductor found an old dog collar by the tracks.
No name tag. No ticket.
"No memory, no ride," said the train.
But the collar started to jingle.
Just a little. Like a dog shaking snow off his ears.
So the train took it. Through the fields. Past the river. To a house at the end of a lane.
An old man was asleep in his chair by the door. Waiting. Like every night.
The collar landed softly in his lap.
In his sleep, he smiled. [softly] "Good boy."
