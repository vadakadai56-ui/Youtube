Every winter... someone builds a hotel out of a frozen river.
And every spring... it melts.
It starts in March. Workers saw the river ice into blocks bigger than a fridge.
The blocks wait out the summer... in a giant freezer.
In November, the cold comes back. So do the builders.
Snow and ice are sprayed over steel moulds.
Then artists carve every room by hand.
The bed is ice. The glasses are ice.
[softly] Inside, it stays below freezing... all night.
Then spring comes. The walls drip. The arches sag.
And the whole hotel flows back into the river...
[warmly] ...where next March, it will be cut out all over again.
