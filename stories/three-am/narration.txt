Every night at three a.m., my dog does something terrifying.
Monday. He stands at the end of my bed. Staring. Not moving.
He was staring at a moth.
Tuesday. Scratching. Inside the wall.
It was the pipes. He was scratching back.
Wednesday. Low growling. At the front door.
It was the post. Twelve hours early. Nobody knows why.
Thursday. I wake up, and he's gone.
The back door is open. Footprints in the snow.
I followed them. Across the garden. Into the shed.
He was sitting on my old beanbag... watching the neighbour's Christmas lights.
So yeah. Every night at three. Terrifying.
