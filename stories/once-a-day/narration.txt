In my town, you can say each word only once a day.
Say it twice... and nothing comes out.
So everyone talks very carefully. "Morning." "Hello." "Greetings." "Hiya."
Except me. My words never run out.
I say "sorry" about fifty times a day. People think I'm showing off.
Then one snowy night, Mrs Pell's dog ran into the woods.
Biscuit only comes when you call him three times.
The whole town stood at the trees. Nobody could do it.
So I stepped forward.
[shouting] "Biscuit! Biscuit! BISCUIT!"
Out of the snow... came one very cold dog.
Now the whole town lets me say "good boy" as many times as I like.
Comment the one word you'd save for the whole day.
