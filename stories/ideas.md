# Story ideas (pitched, not built)

1. **The Snow Globe Town.** "Inside a snow globe in your grandma's attic, it has been Christmas Eve for 52 years." Townsfolk fear "the Shake". A boy finds it, and Grandpa made it 52 years ago for Grandma. Seasonal (December), and snow is ideal for animation. 3 parts.
2. **The 3:03 Train.** "Every night at 3:03, a tiny train takes lost things home. You need a memory, not a ticket." The conductor is the wind-up mouse from the Lost Toy Room finale. Twist: an old dog collar with no ticket, and an old dog waiting by a door. 3 to 4 parts.
3. **The Letters That Never Left.** "Every letter you wrote and never sent… is still waiting in one mailbox." Rule: a letter is delivered only if the writer says it out loud. "Dad, I'm sorry." Twist: it flies to the writer's own child. 2 to 3 parts.
