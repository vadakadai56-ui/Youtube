You discover that every present you wrap... shows you the exact moment it gets opened.
A scarf for Grandma. You see her smile. Christmas morning, nine o'clock.
Socks for Dad. He laughs. Ten past nine.
A robot for your little brother. He screams. Best day ever.
So you wrap everything. Every gift you've ever bought. You've seen every Christmas morning before it happens.
No surprises. No mistakes. Perfect.
Then you wrap the one present you didn't buy.
The one with no name on it. Hidden behind the tree.
You see... nothing.
Just snow. And an empty room.
[whispering] Who is that present for?
