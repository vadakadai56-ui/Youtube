# The Lost Toy Room (4 parts, released 19 Nov 2026, 2 hours apart)

World rule: **every toy you've ever lost ends up in one room. A toy can only leave if someone remembers it.**
Characters: Bun-Bun (a worn stuffed rabbit, crooked stitched ear, 41 years in the room), Robot (a shiny new toy), Teddy (pink), the old woman (Ellie's grandma, who lost Bun-Bun as a child), Ellie (her granddaughter).
Recurring motif: a music-box melody (A minor: 69 72 76 74 72 71 69 64 65 69 72 71 69 68 69 76) that turns out to be grandma's lullaby.

## Part 1: "Every toy you lost is waiting in this room"
First comment: "What was the toy YOU lost? 👇"
```
Every toy you've ever lost... ends up in one room.
There's only one rule. A toy can only leave... if someone remembers it.
Most toys leave in a week.
Some wait a year.
Bun-Bun has waited forty-one years.
Every night, he sits by the door. Just in case.
The new toys laugh. "Nobody's coming for you, old man."
Bun-Bun doesn't argue. He fixes his ear. And he waits.
Then, one Tuesday... the door opens.
An old woman walks in. Slowly. Holding a little girl's hand.
She walks past every shelf... and stops.
[whispering] "Hi, Bun-Bun. I'm sorry I took so long."
And for the first time in forty-one years... Bun-Bun goes home.
That night, the room got a new arrival.
It wasn't a toy. It was a phone.
[quietly] And it was still ringing.
```

## Part 2: "The lost toy room got a phone call… (Part 2)"
First comment: "Who do you think can save her? 👇"
```
Nobody in the Lost Toy Room had ever heard a phone ring.
It rang... for three hours.
Finally, Robot pressed the green button.
A voice crackled. [softly] "It's me... Bun-Bun."
The whole room went silent.
"I need your help," he said. "Ellie lost something today."
"Not a toy. Something bigger."
"Her grandma went for a walk this morning... and never came home."
The toys looked at each other. Grown-ups don't get lost here.
Then... the door creaked.
And there she was.
She looked at the shelves like she'd never seen them before.
[whispering] "Excuse me... do you know... who I am?"
And the rule is the rule. She can only leave... if someone remembers her.
```

## Part 3: "She forgot everyone… except this song (Part 3)"
First comment: "Do you remember a song someone sang to you? 👇"
```
The old woman sat down by the door. Right where Bun-Bun used to wait.
The toys tried everything to help her remember.
Robot showed her a photo. Nothing.
Teddy said her name. Nothing.
Then... the phone rang again.
[softly] "It won't work," Bun-Bun whispered. "Toys can't bring her back. It has to be someone she loves."
"Ellie is looking everywhere. But she doesn't know where this room is."
Then... the old woman started humming.
A soft little song. The toys had never heard it before.
But somewhere far away... a little girl stopped walking.
Because she knew that song.
[quietly] And then... the lightbulb began to flicker.
```

## Part 4: "The lost toy room's last rule (Finale)"
First comment: "Who would YOU go back for? 👇"
```
The bulb went dark. And in the dark... someone knocked.
The door opened. A little girl stood there, out of breath, holding a rabbit with one crooked ear.
[softly] "Grandma?"
Ellie didn't say her name. She sang the song.
And something came back into the old woman's eyes.
[whispering] "Ellie... my Ellie."
Someone remembered her. So the door opened wide.
They walked out together, hand in hand.
But Bun-Bun stayed behind. On purpose.
Because every lost thing deserves someone waiting for it.
And that night... a new toy arrived.
Bun-Bun smiled. [warmly] "Don't worry. Someone always remembers."
```
