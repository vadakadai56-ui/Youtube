Every letter you wrote and never sent... ends up in one old red postbox at the end of my street.
It's full. It's been full for years.
Letters to old friends. Letters to first loves. "I'm sorry" letters.
The postman says there's one rule. A letter only leaves if someone reads it out loud.
So every night, I read one.
"Dear Tom, I should have come to your party."
Whoosh. Gone.
"Dear Mum, I didn't mean it."
Whoosh. Gone.
Then I found one in my dad's handwriting. Addressed to me. Dated the year I was born.
I read it out loud.
[softly] "Dear kid. I'm going to get a lot of things wrong. But I'll never stop trying."
It didn't fly away.
It just sat there. Because it was already home.
