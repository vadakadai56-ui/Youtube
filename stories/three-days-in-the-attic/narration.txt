Day one. You're a toy... in a box marked "give away".
The van comes in three days.
You don't remember getting here.
Only dust. A cold window. And snow.
Then something moves in the dark. [whispering] "Don't be scared."
It's a wind-up mouse. His name is Pip.
He's been in this box the longest.
"Every Friday, a van takes a box away," he says. "And those toys... never come back."
Beside you, a music box ballerina turns... with no music.
She forgot her song a long time ago.
"There's only one way out," Pip says.
"Someone has to remember you. Not just see you... remember you. By name."
So you try. You try to remember your own name.
A red coat. A chipped boot. A child's laugh, very far away.
But the name... won't come.
Thirty years is a long time to be forgotten.
Then... the attic door creaks.
Footsteps. On the ladder.
A torch beam sweeps across the box.
[whispering] "Hide," Pip says.
Do you hide... or do you let her see you?
