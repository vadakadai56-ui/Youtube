Every evening at six... a man walks into the park.
He goes to the same swing.
And he pushes it.
There's no one on it.
He laughs. He talks to the air. [softly] "Higher? Okay... higher!"
The other parents pull their children away.
Someone whispers, [whispering] "His little girl loved that swing... before."
He comes when it rains.
He comes when it snows.
Every evening. Six o'clock. For one hundred and twelve days.
Nobody ever looked across the road.
At the children's hospital. Fourth floor. Third window.
Where a little girl named Lily presses her hand to the glass.
She can't go outside. Not yet.
But every evening at six... she watches her swing fly.
And she laughs. [giggles] "Higher, Daddy... higher!"
Then one evening... in spring... the hospital window was empty.
Because Lily was finally outside.
She climbed on the swing all by herself.
[warmly] "Higher, Daddy."
