You discover that every time you sneeze... the world rewinds ten seconds.
It happens in maths class. You write the wrong answer. You sneeze.
The clock jumps back. The answer's gone. The pencil's back in your hand.
You write the right one.
At lunch, your friend's drink tips over. You sneeze. You catch the cup before it falls.
He stares at you. "How did you know?"
You don't tell him.
Missed shot? Sneeze. Score.
Said something dumb to your crush? Sneeze. Say something smooth instead.
You start sniffing pepper before every test.
Then... winter comes.
You wake up with a cold.
You sneeze. Ten seconds back.
You sneeze again. Ten seconds back.
And again. And again.
Mom walks in with tissues. Again. And again. And again.
It's been the same ten seconds for what feels like a week.
You hold your breath... and it stops.
The clock finally moves forward. You made it.
Then the doorbell rings.
It's Grandma. Holding the biggest bunch of flowers you've ever seen.
[whispering] "...Oh no."
