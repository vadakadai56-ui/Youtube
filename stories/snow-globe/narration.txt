Inside a snow globe in my grandma's attic... it has been Christmas Eve for fifty-two years.
The tiny town never sleeps. The tiny clock never moves.
Every winter, a tiny man stands under the lamp post, waiting.
Every winter, the snow falls. And nobody comes.
I found it in a box marked "Do not shake."
So of course... I shook it.
The snow swirled. The tiny bells rang. And a tiny door opened at last.
A tiny woman stepped out, holding a tiny cake.
She walked to the lamp post. She took his hand.
I carried it downstairs. Grandma went very quiet.
[softly] "Your grandad made that... the Christmas we met."
"He said one day, someone would finally shake it."
She turned it over. On the bottom, in his handwriting:
[warmly] "For Ellie. I waited."
