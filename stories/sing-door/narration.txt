In my town, every door only opens if you sing to it.
Not talk. Sing.
The bakery likes jazz. The school likes opera. The bank only likes sad songs.
Except I can't sing. At all.
When I sing, dogs leave. Windows crack. Doors stay shut.
Then on Christmas Eve, the old bakery door froze solid, with Mr Fenn stuck inside.
The whole town sang. Beautifully. Nothing.
So I stepped up... and I sang the worst note this town has ever heard.
The ice cracked. The door swung open.
Mr Fenn came out holding one warm loaf. "For you," he said. "Please never sing again."
Comment the song you'd sing to your front door.
