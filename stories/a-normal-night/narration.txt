In my town... a normal night is terrifying.
At seven, something scratched at the back door.
Slow. Patient. Scratch... scratch...
I opened it.
It was the cat. She wanted in.
At eight, a face in the window. Pale. Round. Smiling.
It was the snowman. We built him this morning.
At nine... footsteps on the roof. Heavy ones.
Then a crash, right outside my window.
It was snow. Sliding off the roof.
At ten, every light on our street went out. All at once.
A dark figure came up the path.
It was my dad. He'd unplugged the Christmas lights. "Too bright."
At midnight, the phone rang. No caller ID.
I picked up. Breathing. Then a voice.
[whispering] "Did you take my good scissors?"
It was Grandma.
So yeah. Another normal night in Dustlight.
