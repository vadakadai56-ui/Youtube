Grandad's advent calendar has twenty-five little doors.
He made it himself, out of an old cigar box.
Every December, he opens one door a day. A chocolate. A button. A tiny paper star.
But for eleven years... he never opened the last one.
[softly] "Not yet," he says. "Not this year."
My cousins think it's empty. My aunt thinks it's stuck.
I asked him once. He just tapped the little door and smiled.
This year, Grandad couldn't come down the stairs.
So I carried the calendar up to him. Door twenty-five.
He put it in my hands. [warmly] "It was always yours."
Inside was a tiny key. And a note in his wobbly writing.
"For the shed."
In the shed... a brand new box. Twenty-five little doors. Unpainted.
And my name, carved on the lid.
The note said... [warmly] "Your turn. Make one for someone you love."
